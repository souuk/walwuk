# Hybrid engine baseline — 2026-08-11

This report records the first accuracy-first hybrid baseline. It is a
diagnostic sample, not a statistically sufficient promotion test.

## Environment

- Intel Core i5-12600KF, 16 reported logical processors.
- Runtime tournament cap: 12 logical processors.
- Tournament memory cap: 1,536 MiB; two 96 MiB Wasm instances were budgeted
  per match process, limiting the run to eight concurrent pairs.
- Current Wasm SHA-256:
  `225ffbfb42e5fc475117bdf62c54e597cdd542790fc192c1a179f30db5eb99c5`.

## Correctness gates

- Native and TypeScript rule parity passed on 10 curated fixtures and 100,000
  deterministic random legal positions.
- The three-ply all-root audit scored 1,260 legal root moves. The selective
  choice had zero exhaustive regret in all 10 fixtures.
- Native fixed-depth search, split-root search, and TypeScript reference
  results matched through the tested exhaustive depths.
- Pages and extension builds, lint, TypeScript, and extension packaging tests
  passed.

## Competitive match sample

The hybrid and exhaustive engines received equal total compute per move.
Games used deterministic paired openings with colors exchanged.

| limit | games | hybrid | exhaustive | unresolved | avg plies | hybrid avg depth | exhaustive avg depth |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 seconds | 12 | 7 | 5 | 0 | 67.08 | 15.79 | 14.07 |
| 15 seconds | 12 | 6 | 6 | 0 | 58.67 | 14.70 | 12.65 |

Across both controls the hybrid scored 13–11 and reached about 1.72 additional
main plies at 10 seconds and 2.05 at 15 seconds. Twenty-four games are much too
small for an SPRT conclusion; more paired openings are required.

Eight concurrent match processes reduced per-process NPS through CPU
contention. At 10 seconds, hybrid and exhaustive aggregate NPS were roughly
545,000 and 819,000. At 15 seconds they were roughly 623,000 and 965,000.
Hybrid nodes are more expensive because selective extensions reach farther, so
time-to-depth and match outcome are more meaningful than comparing these node
counts directly.

## Curated time-to-depth

At the unloaded 15-second position control, ordinary opening and middlegame
fixtures completed exhaustive depth 6 and selective depths 8–10. The
low-reserve fixture completed exhaustive depth 12 and selective depth 16.
Five of ten fixtures still preferred different moves across the unequal
horizons, confirming that the verifier override is necessary.

## Unloaded throughput

Single-worker exhaustive Wasm throughput on three representative positions:

| position | NPS | completed depth |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| opening | 2,817,648 | 5 |
| channelled routes | 2,166,405 | 5 |
| transposition rich | 2,741,860 | 5 |

A two-second root-split benchmark produced 1,563,163 NPS with one worker and
5,491,206 NPS with four workers. The 3.51× throughput increase is about 87.8%
parallel efficiency, above the program's 60% gate.

## Follow-up

The next promotion test should increase paired openings substantially and run
an SPRT-style sequence at fixed-node, 1-second, 5-second, 10-second, and
15-second controls. Learned ordering, evaluation changes, and additional
pruning remain experimental until they pass those tests independently.
